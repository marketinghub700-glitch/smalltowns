Why this exists

The kind of guide we'd want to find.

Most small-town info online is wrong, stale, or doesn't exist. We try to fix that — one town at a time.

Verified local info

Every guide is cross-checked against local directories, official panchayat records, and ground-truthed with people who actually live there. No AI-generated filler.

Hospitals, schools & shops

The things you actually need to know: which hospital has a 24-hour pharmacy, which school has what board, which shop is open past 9pm. Practical, not promotional.

Updated regularly

Each guide shows when it was last updated. When a bus route changes or a hospital shifts location, we go back and fix it. Stale info is worse than no info.
