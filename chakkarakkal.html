Chakkarakkal: A Detailed Guide to a Township in Kannur District

Overview

Chakkarakkal (ചക്കരക്കൽ) is a small village and local township in Kannur district, in the North Malabar region of Kerala. It falls under the Iriveri Panchayath and lies within the Edakkad Block, roughly 12 km east of Kannur town. Set close to the Arabian Sea coast, the area experiences the humid, tropical climate typical of coastal Kerala, with a pronounced monsoon season from June to September and warm, humid conditions for much of the rest of the year.

Though not a major tourist destination in its own right, Chakkarakkal is a well-connected, self-sufficient local hub. For a settlement of its size, it has a surprising density of everyday amenities — hospitals, banks, shops, eateries, educational institutions, and places of worship — most of them clustered within a kilometre or two of the town centre. This makes it a practical, comfortable base for anyone working, studying, or living in the wider Kannur–Edakkad area, and a convenient stopover for travellers heading to North Kerala's better-known coastal and hill attractions.

Location and Connectivity

Chakkarakkal sits at a genuinely convenient crossroads within Kannur district. It is about 12 km east of Kannur town, 4 km from Edakkad, roughly 15 km from Thalassery and Mattannur, and about 15–16 km from Koothuparamba. This central position means residents can reach several major North Malabar towns within a 20–30 minute drive, without needing to live inside a crowded city centre.

  • By road: National Highway 66 runs through Kannur town, connecting Chakkarakkal northward toward Goa and Mumbai, and southward toward Kochi and Thiruvananthapuram. Local roads such as Moonuperiya–Chakkarakkal Road, Muzhappala Road, and Thaze Chovva–Mattannur Road link the town to its surrounding villages and markets.
  • By rail: The nearest railway stations are Kannur South (~9 km) and Kannur Main/Etakkot (~12 km), both on the Mangalore–Palakkad railway line, offering broad connectivity across South India.
  • By air: Kannur International Airport at Mattanur is the closest airport, with domestic connections across India and a number of international routes, particularly to the Gulf region — reflecting Kannur district's large expatriate population. Calicut and Mangalore international airports are also within a couple of hours' drive.

Local buses connect through several well-used stops in the area, including the Chakkarakkal Hospital Bus Stop on Moonuperiya–Chakkarakkal Road, Appakkadavu Bus Stop, Nalaam Peedika Bus Stop, and Aanenimetta Bus Stop on Muzhappala Road — giving residents multiple points of access rather than a single central stand.

What's Available in Chakkarakkal

Healthcare

Healthcare is one of Chakkarakkal's genuine strengths for a village of its size:

  • Community Health Centre, Iriveri — the main government health facility, on Moonuperiya–Chakkarakkal Road, providing outpatient and basic inpatient care to the local population
  • Government Hospital, Chakkarakkal — also on Moonuperiya–Chakkarakkal Road
  • Pavana Hospital and CMC Medicare — private hospitals and clinics serving the town
  • Ayush Primary Health Centre (Homoeopathy), Chembilode — a government homeopathy centre, reflecting Kerala's strong public investment in AYUSH systems alongside allopathic medicine
  • A short distance away, the Malabar Cancer Centre at Kodiyeri, Thalassery (on Muzhikkara–MCC Road) is a major regional cancer-care institute, drawing patients from across North Kerala and making the wider Chakkarakkal–Thalassery corridor an important healthcare axis for the district

Banking and ATMs

  • Federal Bank ATM and Syndicate Bank ATM, both on or near Chakkarakkal Anjarakandy Road
  • Canara Bank ATM, located inside the Gokulam Kalyana Mandapam building on Hospital Road
  • India One ATM, in the centre of Chakkarakkal

Fuel

Multiple petrol stations serve the town and passing traffic, including a general Petrol Station, the Chakkarakkal Petrol Bunk, and Vijaya Fuels on Chakkarakkal Road, plus the Essar Petrol Station a little further out at Nalampeedika.

Shopping, Supermarkets and Electronics

  • Day 2 Day supermarket on Thaze Chovva–Mattannur Road, part of a well-known Kerala supermarket chain
  • Daymart Hyper, near the town gate on Kannur Road
  • A local grocery outlet known in the area as Kattumadam
  • Computer and electronics repair services clustered near the bus stand, including Laptechs Computer / Laptech System Repair and Service
  • Sahara Plaza Shopping Centre, a well-known local shopping landmark that anchors much of the town's retail activity

Restaurants, Hotels and Lodges

Chakkarakkal has a lively small food and hospitality scene, catering to both residents and travellers passing through on the Kannur–Thalassery corridor:

  • New Saseendra Restaurant, Tavakkal Chicken Stall, Brothers Hotel, Salala Restaurant Fastfood & Coolbar, and Coffee Mug Restocafe are among the popular local eateries, offering everything from quick snacks to full North Malabar-style meals
  • Lodging options include Broad Bean Hotel (near Kannur Medical College), Hotel Dwaraka, Vanitha Hotel, and NN Cottage
  • The Kudumbashree Millenium Vanitha Hotel is worth a special mention — it's run under Kerala's Kudumbashree programme, a state-wide women's self-help-group network that has become a model for community-driven micro-enterprise across the state

Education

  • Jawahar College and Amritha College, located directly in Chakkarakkal
  • Bharath Vidya Mandir, a school within the town
  • Vivekananda Tuition Centre and CAS Tuition Centre, local coaching and tuition institutes supporting school and competitive-exam preparation, a common feature of Kerala's education-focused small towns
  • Nearby schools include Moovanchery U.P. School, Melur Junior Basic School, Mavilayi Central A.L.P. School, A.K.G. Memorial Government Higher Secondary School (Peralasseri), and Anjarakandy Higher Secondary School
  • Deenul Islam Sabha English HSS in Kannur city is within easy commuting distance
  • Further afield, institutions such as the College of Engineering and Technology (Payyanur) and Mary Matha Arts & Science College (Alakode) serve students from across the wider region

Places of Worship

Chakkarakkal's religious landscape reflects the broader diversity of Kannur district, which has significant Hindu, Muslim, and Christian communities living alongside one another:

  • Salafi Masjid, Safa Masjid, and Chakkarakkal Town Juma Masjid, all within the town itself
  • Appakkadavu Sree Muthappan Kshethram, a well-known Muthappan temple about a kilometre away — Muthappan worship is a distinctive North Malabar tradition, closely associated with the region's famous Theyyam ritual performances
  • Paleri Ambalam and the adjoining Paleri Ambala Kulam (temple tank) at nearby Anjarakandy

Cinema

  • Movie Max, a newer multiplex-style theatre located at Sahakari Arena on Chakkarakkal–Anjarakandy Road — a notable recent addition, giving the town its own cinema option rather than residents needing to travel into Kannur city for a movie

Recreation and Nature

  • The Munderi Kadavu Bird Sanctuary, about 7 km away, is a notable birdwatching spot in the wider Edakkad area, drawing nature enthusiasts interested in the wetland and riverine bird species of the Kannur backwaters
  • Local green spaces such as Palli Kaadu lie a short distance out of town

Venues and Local Landmarks

  • Gokulam Kalyana Mandapam, a wedding and event hall on Hospital Road that also houses the Canara Bank ATM — a common small-town arrangement in Kerala, where commercial and banking services are folded into larger community buildings
  • Government offices including the Ayush Primary Health Centre, and — a little further out — the Anjarakandy Panchayath Office and the Anjarakandy CDS FFC (Farmer-Friendly Cooperative) office, which supports local agricultural credit and services

Nearby Tourist Attractions

Chakkarakkal makes a convenient base for visiting several well-known North Kerala destinations:

  • Muzhappilangad Beach (~11 km) — Kerala's only drive-in beach, a roughly 4-km stretch of firm sand where visitors can drive or ride motorcycles along the shoreline, one of the more unusual coastal experiences in the state
  • Kannur town (~11–12 km) — known for its beaches, its historic St. Angelo Fort built by the Portuguese, and as one of the best places in Kerala to witness Theyyam, the elaborate ritual dance-worship tradition unique to North Malabar
  • Thalassery (~15 km) — a historic coastal town famous for introducing bakery culture to Kerala, along with Thalassery Fort and a long-standing circus training tradition that has produced generations of Indian circus performers
  • Ranipuram (~36 km) — a cool, forested hill station popular for trekking and short getaways from the coastal heat
  • Payyoli (~50 km) — a coastal town in neighbouring Kozhikode district, known for its beach and, more recently, as the birthplace of Olympic sprinter P.T. Usha

Surrounding Villages

Chakkarakkal is bordered by small villages including Chembilode (~4 km), Chelora (~5 km), Munderi (~6 km), Peralasseri (~7 km), and Keezhallur (~7 km). Kannur Block lies to the west, Thalassery Block to the south, Kuthuparamba Block to the east, and Irikkur Block to the north — placing Chakkarakkal roughly at the centre of this cluster of blocks.

Summary

Chakkarakkal is a compact, well-connected North Kerala village that punches above its weight in day-to-day amenities. Hospitals, banks, supermarkets, eateries, lodges, colleges, and places of worship are all within walking or short driving distance of the town centre, while major regional attractions like Muzhappilangad Beach, historic Thalassery, and Kannur's Theyyam heritage are all within easy reach. For residents, it offers small-town convenience with big-town access; for visitors, it's a practical, low-key base for exploring North Malabar.

Note: Business names, addresses, and contact details for small-town establishments can change over time. For the most current details, local directories, Google Maps, or the Iriveri Panchayath office are good places to verify specifics before visiting.
